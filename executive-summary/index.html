Executive summary

Written 20 November 2026 by session arena-04112b09, after a line-by-line pass over the brief, the repository, and six official pages.

1 · The situation in one paragraph

The brief asked for a compliant, traffic-worthy Amazon affiliate publication built around five products, plus the automation to research, publish and optimise it at scale. The repository was empty when this session started — one commit, a six-byte README — so there was no prior source register to polish. Of the five product identifiers supplied, none can currently be resolved to a listing, which means the only honest product page is a register of what is missing. Everything else the brief asked for — a shop-like front page, question-shaped guides, disclosure above the fold, sponsored links, sitemap and feed, a measurement plan, an empty-but-defined campaign ledger, and a gate that refuses unsourced claims — is built and live.

3 · The three facts that changed the plan

  1. Prices and ratings are not free data. They may be shown only where Amazon serves the link or from the approved API, and review/star display is tied to that same API. Result: no number in any currency appears anywhere on this site, and the build fails if one is added by hand.
  2. The API that would power “automated research” is behind a sales gate. Creators API requires an enrolled account and at least ten qualifying sales in the last thirty days; PA-API 5 is deprecated and returns 403. Result: automation is sequenced after content earns, not before — and the ledger stays empty rather than predictive.
  3. Some obvious affiliate tricks are explicitly forbidden. Price alerts, per-visitor sub-tagging, incentivised clicks, pop-ups, redirect intermediaries and search-engine placements on “amazon” keywords each have a clause. Result: they are absent by construction, and the gate asserts their absence.

4 · What is blocking the commercial half

Three blocking questions, all answerable by the account owner in under fifteen minutes: which product each link points at (Q-01), whether the identifier tokens are truncated (Q-02), and which marketplace the tracking ID is registered against (Q-03). Until those are answered, the site can publish method but not verdicts — which is exactly the distinction the guides are built on.

6 · What this summary does not claim

  • It does not claim traffic, rankings, conversions or revenue. There is no measurement history in this repository, and the ledger is empty on purpose.
  • It does not claim the five supplied links are invalid. Their compliance status is unresolved and stays flagged.
  • It does not claim any product is good, bad, or suitable for you. No product has been confirmed, let alone tested.
  • It does not present the ASIN length rule as verified from an official page; we reported a measurement, not a verdict from a source we could not open.

Everything above is reproducible: the data files are published next to the HTML, the quotes are in the register, and npm run verify re-runs the whole argument.
