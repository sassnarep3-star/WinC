Your first air fryer: the six checks before you buy

A first-time buyer's checklist: six things to compare, each one a spec you can read on the listing before you spend anything.

By the ShopNow Kitchen Desk · Published 2026-11-20 · Updated 2026-11-20 · 2 min read · This category · Sources

Buying your first air fryer is not a research project. It is six checks, and every one of them is a line you can read on the listing itself — no reviews to decode, no "top 10" to trust. This page walks the six checks in the order that matters, using the three air fryers in this shop as worked examples. Which one is right depends on your kitchen, not on a ranking.

The six checks

#CheckHow to do it in one minuteSource
1Count the basketsA dual-basket unit (two 3-Qt baskets, B0CDY277D3) cooks a main and a side at once; a single-basket unit (B08DKYBTPH) cooks one bigger batch. Neither is better — they answer different dinners.B0CDY277D3 · B08DKYBTPH
2Match the capacity to your batchThe listings state 6 quarts (as two 3-Qt baskets), 8 quarts (single basket) and a 26-quart oven cavity (B09B7SB46R). Write down the meal you cook most and check it against the number, not the marketing.B0CDY277D3 · B08DKYBTPH · B09B7SB46R
3Measure the counter firstThe oven-style unit lists a 16.9-inch depth — measure counter depth and cabinet clearance before anything else. The basket units list roughly 13 inches.B09B7SB46R · B0CDY277D3 · B08DKYBTPH
4Weigh the storageListed weights: 13.3 lb (B0CDY277D3), 12.8 lb (B08DKYBTPH), 26.5 lb (B09B7SB46R). The last one is about double — plan where it lives when it is not in use.B0CDY277D3 · B08DKYBTPH · B09B7SB46R
5Read the functions, not the preset countOne listing's title and bullets disagree on its own preset count (24 vs 25 — FLG-015), which is a good reminder: the number is marketing. Look for the functions you will actually use weekly.B09B7SB46R
6Check price and availability on Amazon, not hereThis site does not display prices — Amazon's own policies are the reason, and the listing shows the live figure anyway.Amazon Program Policies (S02)
