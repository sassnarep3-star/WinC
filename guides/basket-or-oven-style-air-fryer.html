Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?

Two baskets that finish together vs. one big oven cavity. Compare capacity, footprint, and cleanup across the three air fryers in the shop.

By the ShopNow Kitchen Desk · Updated 2026-11-20 · 4 min read · Sources

Three air fryers are in the shop, and they split into two shapes. Two are basket-style (one or two removable baskets you slide in and out). One is oven-style (a countertop oven cavity with French doors and racks). The shape changes everything about capacity, cleanup, and counter space — so compare the shape first, then the specs.

Which shape fits your kitchen?

  • Choose basket-style if you cook two foods at once (main + side), want the smallest footprint, and prefer fewer parts to wash.
  • Choose oven-style if you need rack space for baking, rotisserie, or a full tray — and you have the counter depth for a 16.9-inch-deep unit.
  • Either way, measure your counter and the cabinet above it before you order. Depth is where oven-style units surprise people.

Frequently asked questions

Is a 6-quart dual basket the same as a 6-quart single basket?

No. The Chefman 6 Qt splits capacity into two 3-Qt baskets with a Sync Finish option, so two foods can finish together. A single 6-Qt basket gives one larger space. Compare how you actually cook: two small batches, or one big one?

Why is the oven-style model so much heavier?

Per its listing it weighs 26.5 pounds, roughly double the basket models, because it is a full countertop oven with racks and accessories (7 included per listing). Weight matters for where it lives and how you clean around it.

Do I need windows to see the food?

The Chefman 6 Qt has easy-view windows on each basket; the Chefman 8 Qt listing does not mention windows. Windows help you check doneness without pulling the basket — a small thing that matters if you cook often.
