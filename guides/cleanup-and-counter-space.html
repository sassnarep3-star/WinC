How to assess cleanup and counter-space trade-offs

Dishwasher-safe parts, windows, and the inches your counter actually has. A practical cleanup and footprint guide for basket vs. oven-style fryers.

By the ShopNow Kitchen Desk · Updated 2026-11-20 · 4 min read · Sources

Air fryers promise less oil. They do not promise less washing. Cleanup and counter space are where “great on paper” appliances end up in a closet. Compare these two things before you buy.

Counter space: measure depth first

  • Chefman 6 Qt: 13.38 in deep × 11.81 in wide × 10.63 in tall.
  • Chefman 8 Qt: 12.75 in deep × 13.5 in wide × 10 in tall.
  • Emeril 26QT: 16.9 in deep × 15.8 in wide × 14.8 in tall — measure counter depth and the cabinet above it.

Tip: tape the footprint on your counter with painter’s tape. Leave it for a day. If you keep moving it, it is too big for that spot.

Windows and shake reminders: small features, daily use

The Chefman 6 Qt has easy-view windows and a shake reminder; the 8 Qt has an LED shake reminder. These do not change capacity — they change how often you open the basket to check. If you cook daily, they matter. If you cook weekly, they do not.

Frequently asked questions

Are all parts dishwasher-safe?

Listings say baskets/parts are dishwasher-safe, but always confirm which parts on Amazon — windows, seals, and accessories are often hand-wash only.

How much counter depth do I need?

At least the unit’s depth plus a few inches to pull baskets or open doors. The Emeril needs ~17 inches of depth; many counters are 24–25 inches deep, but cabinets above can be the real limit.
