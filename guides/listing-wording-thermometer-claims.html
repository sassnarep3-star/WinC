“Unlimited range” and “IP67 waterproof”: how to read thermometer listing copy without over-trusting it

Two phrases that sell wireless thermometers, what a listing does and does not commit to, and the USDA FSIS care rules that a waterproof rating does not override. A reading guide, not a review.

By the ShopNow Kitchen Desk · Published 2026-11-20 · Updated 2026-11-20 · 3 min read · This category · Sources

Thermometer listings are written to win a search, not to survive a close reading. That is not dishonest — it is a different purpose. The useful skill is knowing which phrase commits to something checkable and which one is doing atmosphere. Three examples, taken from a listing on this shelf.

1. “WiFi unlimited range”

The CHEF iQ Sense listing title reads “3 Ultra-Thin Probes | WiFi Unlimited Range | 1000°F Heat Safe | 5 Sensors”. The range phrase describes how a reading gets to you, not what happens to your dinner: a network-reported thermometer is only as good as the router, the hub's charge, and whether the alert reaches the person standing away from the oven. The listing does not say what happens when any of those fails, so the pre-purchase question is concrete and answerable only from the manual or the manufacturer's own page: does the base unit raise its own alarm, and does the alert survive a phone on silent in another room?

Sources: CHEF iQ Sense listing title · read 2026-11-20.

2. “IP67 waterproof” — and what FSIS says about washing a thermometer

The ThermoMaven listing carries “IP67 Waterproof” in its title. An ingress rating is a statement about the enclosure; it is not an instruction to clean the instrument however you like. USDA FSIS's thermometer care guidance is stricter and worth reading against it: “As with any cooking utensil, food thermometers should be washed with hot, soapy water or according to the manufacturer's instructions. Most thermometers should not be immersed in water. Wash carefully by hand.”

That is the gap a buyer can close in one minute: does the manufacturer's own manual permit rinsing the probe under running water, and does it say the unit may be immersed? If the manual and the rating disagree, the manual is the document that governs whether your warranty survives Tuesday's cleanup.

3. “Leave-in” is a specification, not a vibe

FSIS draws the line the listings often blur: “Do not leave your food thermometer in food while cooking unless the manufacturer's instructions specifically say it can withstand heat and has been designed for this purpose.” Its types table then separates the categories: wireless/smart thermometers are described as designed to remain in the food while cooking, inserted at least 1 inch deep in the thickest part of the food, providing live status on internal temperature, and “more costly”. Instant-read styles (FSIS lists thermistors at 10 seconds and thermocouples at 2–5 seconds) are marked as not designed to remain in the food while cooking.

So the practical read on a listing is: does it say the probe stays in through the cook, and does it state a heat limit for the cable as well as the tip? A 1000°F figure on a probe says nothing about the cable that runs out of your oven door, and it certainly says nothing about the plastic face of a display FSIS warns can melt if placed too close to heat.

A checklist for reading any thermometer listing

Phrase on the listingWhat it can supportThe question it cannot answer
“WiFi unlimited range”The reporting path — network rather than line-of-sight Bluetooth distanceWhat happens to the alarm when the network, the hub's charge or the phone drops
“IP67 waterproof”An enclosure rating, per the manufacturerWhether the manual permits immersion or a running-water rinse, which is what FSIS tells you to check
“1000°F heat safe”A stated heat limit for the part the listing namesThe cable's limit, and whether the display face can sit near that heat
“±0.5℉ accuracy”A published specification you can testYour unit's actual offset — run the ice-cup check
“NIST&NSF Certified”A certification claim to verify with the named bodyNothing this site can confirm from the listing page alone
