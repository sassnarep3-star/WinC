Will it fit? Four measurements to take before an air fryer arrives

One listing says 13.4 inches deep, another says 16.9 inches and 26.5 pounds. Take these four measurements first — the returns desk is not a plan.

By the ShopNow Kitchen Desk · Published 2026-11-20 · Updated 2026-11-20 · 3 min read · This category · Sources

Every number in this guide is a number the manufacturer put on its own Amazon listing, and every one of them is bigger than most people expect. Nobody here has squeezed an appliance into a cupboard — that is the point: the listing is the only measurement source you can cite, so measure your kitchen against it.

The two details a spec table never tells you

  • What opens, and in which direction. Two of these are drawers that pull straight out; the French-door listing names twin doors. Front clearance is measured with the door open, or you have bought a machine you cannot load.
  • Whether the dimensions belong to your variant. One listing offers a size selector, so the depth on the page may describe a different capacity than the one you order. Re-read the dimensions on the exact variant your link lands on before you trust this table — the shop's own flagged list records why that caveat exists.
What this page deliberately does not say: no ventilation clearance figure, no “leave 2 inches” rule, no cabinet-material warning. None of the three listings states one in the fields captured, and an invented number is worse than a blank — go to the manufacturer's manual for the clearance your unit requires. This is the same rule that keeps prices and ratings blank throughout this site.

A cheap way to test the fit before you order

Cut a piece of cardboard to the listing's footprint and set it where the appliance would live. Open a door on the drawn hinge side. Stand under the cabinet with a ruler. It takes five minutes, costs nothing, and it is the same measurement a return label costs you twice.

Then check the food, not the quarts

Fit is the first filter; the second is whether your usual pan or bird actually goes inside. That is a different checklist — quarts are not servings, and two 3-quart baskets are not a 6-quart basket — so it has its own page: the capacity checklist and cleanup and counter-space trade-offs.
