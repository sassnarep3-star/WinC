Black Friday air fryer deals: what should you check before you click buy?

A pre-click checklist for deal season: which size is on the page, what the listing contradicts about itself, and why this site shows no deal price at all.

By the ShopNow Kitchen Desk · Published 2026-11-20 · Updated 2026-11-20 · 3 min read · This category · Sources

Deal season multiplies pressure at exactly the moment listings are most likely to confuse you: size variants, newer-model notes, and countdown language. This page is a pre-click checklist. It names what to verify on the Amazon page itself before any checkout — and it explains why this site, deliberately, shows no deal price.

Why no price appears on this page

The Amazon Associates Program Policies state that because prices and availability change, a participating site "may only show prices and availability if: (a) we serve the link in which that price and availability data are displayed, or (b) you obtain Product pricing and availability data via Creators API or PA API." This site does neither — it is a static page, so every price decision happens on Amazon, where the number is live. The same policy requires sites to remove references to limited-time promotions as soon as the promotion ends, which is why this guide talks about checks, not about a deal price or a countdown.

The pre-click checklist

Check on the listing pageWhy (what this site observed)
Which size is actually selected? Open the size/variant selector and read the title again with the variant chosen.The 8-quart Chefman ASIN (B08DKYBTPH) served with a variation parameter and offers 6, 8 and 9-quart size variants under one page (flagged FLG-023). The wrong variant is a different appliance.
Is there a "newer model" note? Amazon sometimes flags one above the buy box.The same 8-quart page states a newer model of that item exists (flagged FLG-024). If the newer one is what you wanted, buy that one on purpose, not by accident.
Do the title and bullets agree on numbers?The Emeril 26QT page's title says 24 one-touch presets while its bullet copy says 25 (flagged FLG-020), and its title says six accessories while bullets say seven (flagged FLG-010). Whichever number you care about, read it off the live page.
What temperature range does the page state?The 8-quart Chefman's title advertises a 450°F Hi-Fry option while its bullet copy states a 200–400°F range (flagged FLG-005). If high-heat searing is your use, confirm the range on the page before you buy.
Does the link carry the tag? The URL should contain the associate tag parameter.Special Links must include the Associates ID or "tag" (XXXXX-##) as a parameter (Program Policies). Every buy button on this site carries tag=shopnow047c-20 and is marked Sponsored · paid affiliate link.
Measure the footprint against your counter.Listed footprints differ sharply: 12.75"D × 13.5"W × 10"H (8 Qt), 13.38"D × 11.81"W × 10.63"H (6 Qt dual), 16.9"D × 15.8"W × 14.8"H (26 QT oven). Deal excitement does not add inches.

What a deal page cannot tell you (and we will not invent)

A discount is a comparison between two prices; this site cannot display either one, so it makes no deal claim about any product here — no "best price", no percentage, no "lowest ever". The comparison this site can make is the one between listings: capacity shape, footprint, cleanup, and the contradictions flagged above. If the deal matters more than the spec, Amazon's own page is the place the number lives, on the day you look.

What this page cannot tell you. No price, rating or review count appears on this site, and nobody here has used the products, so no testing or deal claim is made. Every figure is linked to the page it was read from.
