Questions to ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance

24 functions sound great — until you ask which ones you will use weekly. Questions for oven-style fryers and wireless thermometers.

By the ShopNow Kitchen Desk · Published 2026-11-20 · Updated 2026-11-20 · 2 min read · This category · Sources

“24 cooking functions” and “5 sensors” are real specs — and easy to overvalue. Before you buy a multi-function appliance, ask which functions you will use every week, and what the extra complexity costs you in cleanup, counter space, and learning curve.

Ask these seven questions

#QuestionWhy it matters
1Which 3 functions will I use weekly?Emeril lists 24 presets (air fry, bake, broil, dehydrate, roast, slow cook…). Name your three before you buy.
2What do the accessories cost me?7 accessories = 7 things to wash and store. Count them.
3Does it replace something I own?An oven-style fryer can replace a toaster oven — but only if it fits your counter (16.9 in deep).
4For thermometers: probe or wireless?ThermoMaven is a single instant-read probe (±0.5°F). CHEF iQ Sense is 3 wireless probes + hub with WiFi and app alerts.
5What does “wireless” require?CHEF iQ needs WiFi + app + hub charging. ThermoMaven needs a battery and works anywhere. Match to your cooking (grill vs. oven).
6What is the heat limit?CHEF iQ lists 1000°F heat-safe. Confirm the limit for your grill/smoker on Amazon.
7What will I check on Amazon?Price, availability, and “dishwasher-safe” wording live on Amazon. This site does not show prices (policy).

Frequently asked questions

Do I need 24 functions?

Only if you will use them. Most households use 3–5 weekly. If your three are air fry, bake, and roast, a simpler basket model may serve you better than a 24-function oven.

Is wireless always better than instant-read?

No. Wireless wins for long, unattended cooks. Instant-read wins for quick, portable checks and needs no app or WiFi. They solve different problems.
