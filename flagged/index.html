FLG-001 All five supplied identifiers are 9 characters, not 10 severity: blocking for product claims; harmless to the rest of the site

Observed
B04G1MEwY, B02NbDxp3, B00hJ57DV, B0c8moW59, B0f3GKalj each measure 9 characters and mix upper and lower case (`awk`-style measurement recorded in SRC-12, reproducible with `npm run check`).
Why it matters
The identifier is the only thing that makes a product claim checkable. If it is one character short, no listing can be cited honestly, and a well-meaning repair would fabricate a product identity.
What we did
Recorded the length, refused to auto-correct, and left title/listing/price blank. The generator's shape check fails loudly rather than silently passing these tokens through.
What we did not do
We did not confirm the length rule from an official page. Seller Central help was unreachable from this environment (SRC-13 is logged as referenced-not-fetched), so this is reported as an observation and a question, not a ruling.
Resolution
Answer Q-01/Q-02 by supplying the listing URLs or regenerating links from the listings. The build then fills itself and this flag closes.

FLG-003 Compliance status of the supplied short links is unresolved severity: high for the link layer; zero for readers today

Observed
Host is `link.amazon`, not an Amazon Site host we were told to treat as canonical, and the URLs carry no visible `tag=` parameter. We could not observe what they resolve to: egress from this sandbox to those hosts failed with curl error 35, and the platform fetcher errored on the product path.
Why it matters
Two rules bear on it - only Amazon's tagged formats may be used (SRC-01 §1), and a shortening presentation that makes the destination unclear is not permitted (SRC-02 §6(w)). Whether these links violate either depends on what they redirect to and how they were generated.
What we did
Left the question open, exactly as the brief instructs: 'do not claim that the supplied short links are invalid'. We cite the rules, state what we could not observe, and route the site's own outbound links through tagged formats so the site is safe regardless of how this resolves.
Resolution
Run one link through a browser while signed in to the account and record the final URL in the observed_redirect_target field (kept deliberately separate from the intended-ASIN fields).

FLG-004 Tracking ID accepted as given, not as verified severity: blocking for revenue attribution

`shopnow047c-20` is the only ID supplied. It matches the documented `XXXXX-##` shape and contains no Amazon mark.

What we did

Resolution

FLG-005 No prices, ratings or review text appear anywhere, by design severity: expected, informational

Observed
Both are permitted only through an Amazon-served link or Creators/PA API output (SRC-02 §2(b), §6(t)), and Creators API access itself needs at least 10 qualifying sales in the past 30 days (SRC-05).
Why it matters
A single stale price or copied star count is the easiest way for an otherwise careful site to breach the terms and mislead a buyer.
What we did
Blank cells with an unverified marker, plus gate G-06 which scans generated HTML for currency and rating patterns and fails the build.
Resolution
Unlocks with Q-05. Until then the product card's job is to tell readers what to check on the listing, not to report numbers.

FLG-007 'AI-powered platform' needs re-scoping against two hard lines severity: high

Observed
Special Links and Program Content may not be used to develop machine learning models without Amazon's prior written approval (SRC-02 §5); PA-API 5 is deprecated and returns 403 (SRC-06); and synthetic clicks or generated sessions are prohibited (§6(s)).
Why it matters
The natural reading of the brief - an agent that pulls catalogue data, learns from it, and drives traffic automatically - crosses a permission line and rests on a retired endpoint.
What we did
Split the pipeline: generation, formatting, sitemap/feed, disclosure placement and staleness queues are automated in-repo; product identity, claims, and anything touching Program Content for training sit behind a human gate. Documented on the How it works page.
Resolution
Decide whether to request written approval, or to keep machine assistance strictly on original editorial text. Either is workable; both are cheaper than an assumption.

FLG-008 A static host cannot receive the events it defines severity: medium

Observed
Pages serves files only; there is nowhere to POST a click. GitHub Pages separately logs visitor IPs for security (SRC-09), so 'nothing is collected' would be inaccurate.
Why it matters
The brief asks for a self-hosted analytics endpoint. Shipping a form that silently discards submissions, or claiming aggregation we cannot perform, are both worse than saying the collector is disabled.
What we did
Counter runs in the visitor's browser, sums locally, and posts only if `endpoint` is configured in site.config.json (it is null). A sample collector is included as code to run on infrastructure the operator owns.
Resolution
Choose A, B or C in SG-06; then answer Q-11 for the retention and legal basis before any data flows.

FLG-010 Every file in `main` becomes public, including the registers severity: medium

Observed
The Pages source is branch `main` at path `/` with a legacy build, so `data/*.json` is served alongside the HTML unless excluded.
Why it matters
That is a feature for auditability and a leak for anything sensitive. It also means Jekyll-ignored names (`_`, `.`) behave differently from plain static hosts.
What we did
Kept the data readable and secret-free by policy; recorded the tracking ID there anyway because an ID in a page's href is public regardless. `_config.yml` documents the exclusion choices.
Resolution
Confirm that publishing the source register and ledger is wanted. If not, we move data out of the published tree and keep only generated HTML at the root.

FLG-011 Audience locale and store locale may not match severity: high

Observed
The operator is in the Netherlands; the tracking ID pattern is the US program's; no marketplace was stated in the brief.
Why it matters
Sending NL readers to a US listing produces shipping, VAT and warranty friction that no layout can fix, and a tag from a locale that is not enrolled can simply fail to accrue.
What we did
Wrote guides that are store-agnostic, flagged the open question (Q-03), and refused to build silent geo-redirects, which would also be a Redirecting Link.
Resolution
Confirm enrolled locales, then pick SG-03's option and let the generator carry the host.
