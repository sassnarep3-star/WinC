About & editorial policy

Published by Kitchen Shortlist Editorial·Policy version 1.0 · 20 November 2026

Kitchen Shortlist is a buyer-first publication about small kitchen appliances — air fryers, toaster ovens, thermometers — written for people who want to compare two things in ten minutes and then be left alone. We earn commissions when readers buy through our links; that relationship is disclosed on every page, above the fold, in the exact wording Amazon requires and beyond what the FTC asks for.

Who writes this

Bylines currently read Kitchen Shortlist Editorial — an organization-level authorship while the human author identity and contact channel are being finalized (tracked as Q-13 / SG-22). When a named author and corrections address are confirmed, they appear here and in the footer. We'd rather publish that gap than invent a persona.

How topics are chosen

  1. Phase 1 (now): four editorial hypotheses supplied by the project brief — published as working titles, explicitly not claims of popularity, search volume or product superiority.
  2. Phase 2 (after 30 days of data): next topics chosen from measured queries (Search Console + on-site search events), never from instinct. See SG-20.

How claims get sourced

  • Every table row on this site links an official source or is explicitly an empty state — enforced by an automated check on every push.
  • Policy claims (what Amazon and the FTC require) are verified against the primary documents and logged with dates in the source register.
  • Observations (redirects, fetch results) are logged separately from verified claims, with the evidence page linked.
  • Values that can't be confirmed stay blank and labelled unverified. We do not infer, estimate or backfill.

What we refuse to publish

  • Prices, star ratings or commission rates without a policy-compliant, citable source.
  • Claims of popularity, "trending" status or superiority without measured data.
  • Any statement that a supplied link is "invalid" when its status is unresolved — flags say unresolved, not invalid.
  • Modeled conversion scores or revenue forecasts. The ledger waits for real reports.

Corrections

Found an error or a broken source? The corrections channel is being finalized (SG-22) — until it's live, issues can be raised on the repository. Corrections are logged with dates on the affected page; resolved flags stay visible on the flag page with their resolution notes.

How this site is run

Static pages on GitHub Pages, no trackers, no cookies, first-party measurement only (see privacy). Changes flow through a pull request with automated compliance checks — the same gate described on the compliance page. The shared task board for parallel work is COORDINATION.md in the repository.

Editorial independence, stated plainly

Commission potential never decides what we recommend — only what we're allowed to display and how we're paid. The comparison frameworks stay the same whether a link earns nothing or something.
