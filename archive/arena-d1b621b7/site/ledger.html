Entry rules (from the schema):
  1. Zero rows until an authorized first-party report supplies the numbers.
  2. Every row requires timestamp, source, channel, eligible sessions, clicks, reported orders, provenance.
  3. Values are entered exactly as reported, with the report date recorded.
  4. Append-only: corrections are new rows referencing the old one.
  5. Never contains personally identifiable visitor data.
Why so strict? The brief's priority-4 rule: measure before automating optimization. A ledger that starts empty and dated stays honest; a ledger seeded with estimates becomes the source of every future bad decision. Related: event taxonomy · FLG-005 (rates unverified).
