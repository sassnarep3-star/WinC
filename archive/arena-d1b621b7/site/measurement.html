The five events

awaiting approval · SG-13

The brief specified "the five defined events" without a definition in the repo, so this taxonomy was proposed and flagged for approval rather than silently assumed (FLG-009). Conversions are deliberately not an on-site event — orders only enter the system through authorized Associates reports in the campaign ledger.

Event taxonomy · allowlisted in analytics.js; anything else is dropped
Event Fires when Payload Why it exists
page_view Any page finishes loading title, path, referrer (truncated) Denominator for everything; guide popularity after 30 days
outbound_product_click Click on a sponsored product link (link.amazon or /dp/) label, href (truncated) The outbound-click counter — the core revenue signal
guide_cta_click Click on a primary CTA button (shortlist, guides) label, href Measures whether guides route readers to the shop surface
search_query Reader runs the on-page search filter q, results count On-site demand signal before Search Console exists
subscribe_click Click on RSS / subscribe links href Owned-audience growth (SG-30)
What we never collect: no cookies, no raw IP addresses, no names, no cross-site identifiers, no ad-profile data. Session ids are random and stored only in this browser. See the privacy page and Amazon's own privacy-disclosure requirements for associates.
