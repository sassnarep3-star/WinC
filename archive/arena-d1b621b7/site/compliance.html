Special Links status & the one-link test

unresolved · FLG-003
Where things stand. Amazon pays only on properly formatted “Special Links” (V-02), disclaims fees when links aren't correctly tracked (V-05), and prohibits shortening that obscures the Amazon destination (V-08). The supplied link.amazon/… links demonstrably reach Amazon listings (O-01…O-05), but their internal tagging is not observable from outside the Associates account. We do not claim they are invalid — their exact compliance status is unresolved in FLG-003.
The test plan (SG-19) — run inside the enrolled Associates workflow, one link per product:
  1. Open Associates Central → check tracking ID shopnow047c-20 exists (FLG-004).
  2. Generate one official link per product with the Site Stripe / link tools.
  3. Click each supplied link in a clean session; record the final URL including tag=.
  4. Confirm the click appears in the Associates tracking report within 24 h (V-04).
  5. Log results in FLG-003 → then SG-06 link swap can proceed.
