Decision desk

A clear line between what we know, what we don't, and what would make a responsible next decision possible. The proposals below are editorial hypotheses, not claims about demand or product quality.

Executive summary

This repository initially contained only a six-byte README and no product research, source register, site, analytics, or workflow. Five short URLs and the Associates tracking ID were supplied in the brief. Product identities, ASINs, prices, features, availability, commissions, redirect targets and link compliance remain unverified. This site therefore presents question-led buying guidance and an explicitly unverified shortlist—not product rankings or performance claims.

Recommended next path: verify each product and link in the enrolled Associates workflow; gather official manufacturer/listing specifications; then build evidence-backed comparison pages. Do not estimate conversion or revenue from absent data.

Suggestions to choose from

  1. Evidence-first product cards: add official identity, dated source, price only when confirmed, and a blank/unknown state for everything else.
  2. Question-led guide series: develop the four current working titles into original, useful guides after fact gathering. Treat them as hypotheses, not SEO guarantees.
  3. Manual compliance gate: require source links, disclosure review, and Associates link test record before a page can publish.
  4. Measure before optimizing: begin with empty ledgers; use authorized data only. No revenue forecasts or conversion scores until reports exist.
  5. Publication polish: add named author/reviewer, editorial policy, dated updates, accessible imagery with provenance, category navigation, and RSS once there is real publishing cadence.

Decision needed: select a target market and audience, then authorize the source-verification workflow and decide which guide addresses the most useful reader question.
