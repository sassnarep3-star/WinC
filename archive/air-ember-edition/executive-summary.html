The headline irregularities

  • Supplied codes aren’t ASINs (9 characters; Amazon’s rule is 10) — intent fields left blank, FLG-001/003.
  • link.amazon format isn’t documented in the Associates agreement — status unresolved, explicitly not claimed invalid, FLG-002. Buyer CTAs use the documented tagged /dp/?tag= format instead.
  • Tracking ID unvalidated (no account access), FLG-004 — one-link-per-product test (S-02) is the gate before scaling.
  • No conversion data exists — ledger shipped empty, FLG-006. No revenue or conversion claims appear anywhere.

All eight flags with evidence: Flagged for review.
