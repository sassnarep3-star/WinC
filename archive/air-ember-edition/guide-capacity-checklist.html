Checklist

A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

“6 quarts” can mean one basket, two baskets, or marketing rounding. Here’s the two-minute read that cuts through it — run it on any listing, including ours.

Air & Ember desk20 Nov 20267 min read

Capacity is the number most likely to be true and misleading at the same time. It’s usually accurate; it’s just measured in a way that doesn’t map to your dinner. This checklist keeps you honest in six steps.

The checklist

  1. Find the capacity row in the spec table, not the headline. Headlines round; spec tables state units. On our 6 qt dual-basket listing, the spec table literally shows both Capacity: 6 liters and Size: 6 Quart — when a listing shows both units, note both and move on; don’t pick the one that sounds bigger. (Source ↗)
  2. Ask: single or split? A dual-basket 6 qt is two 3 qt zones (the listing says “2 Independent 3Qt” baskets). You can’t put a whole chicken in one of them. A single 8 qt basket gives you one uninterrupted space. Same-ish number, different cooking.
  3. Convert once, then ignore the units. 1 qt ≈ 0.946 L. Our picks: 6 qt ≈ 5.7 L, 8 qt ≈ 7.6 L, 26 qt ≈ 24.6 L — the 26 qt listing itself shows 24.6 liters in its spec table, which is the conversion done for you. (Source ↗)
  4. Match capacity to your pan, not your household. The question isn’t “how many quarts for four people?” — it’s “what’s the largest thing I’ll actually cook?” Frozen fries for four? A 12″ pizza? Wings for two? The listing’s “About this item” bullets usually state what fits (our oven-style listing says 12″ pizza, 6-lb chicken).
  5. Check the ratings density next to the claim. A capacity claim with 16,892 ratings behind it has been stress-tested by more kitchens than a claim with 40. Ratings ≠ proof of capacity, but a thin review count means fewer real-world data points. (Our register has the counts →)
  6. Return-test the claim. Most major retailers state a return window on the order page; Amazon’s own holiday return notice on our observed pages said eligible Nov 1–Dec 31, 2026 purchases can be returned until Jan 31, 2027. Buy, cook one real meal, and if the capacity doesn’t match your life, that’s what the window is for.
Capacity in one line: quarts tell you volume; baskets tell you what fits; your largest regular meal tells you what to buy.
