Q-04 · What does disclosure require, and where?

The FTC’s guidance says disclosure must be placed with the endorsement, hard to miss, in simple language (source ↗). Amazon’s Program Policies require Special Links to carry the Associates tag (source ↗). Implemented as: strip under the masthead on every page, footer repeat, rel="sponsored" + visible tags — details.
