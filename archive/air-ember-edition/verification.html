The verification log

How this site was checked, line by line: the method, the official sources, the dates, and the rule that any value we couldn’t confirm stays blank and gets flagged instead.

Method

  1. Resolve, don’t assume. Each supplied link was fetched live on 2026-11-20 and its destination recorded as the observed redirect target. Supplied codes were never treated as ASINs.
  2. Read the official page, field by field. Title, price, list price, rating, review count, badges, category breadcrumb, and spec rows were read from each amazon.com/dp/… detail page — not from memory, not from third-party aggregators.
  3. Quote policies from the policy owner. Commission rates from Amazon’s rate card; link rules from the Operating Agreement & Program Policies; disclosure rules from the FTC.
  4. Blank beats guessed. Any value not confirmed on an official page on the observation date stays blank, marked ✱, and listed on Flagged for review.
  5. Date everything. Prices and ratings are volatile; every surface shows the observation date and links the live listing for re-checking.
  6. Keep intent and observation in separate fields. Never merged, never back-filled from each other.

What “unverified” means here

Blank + flagged

Value not confirmable from an official source on the observation date → cell stays empty, ✱ marker, listed on Flagged.

Dated snapshot

Value confirmed but volatile (price, rating) → published with its date and a link to the live page for re-checking.

Claim, labelled

Manufacturer statements (e.g. “NIST certified”) → reproduced as listing claims with the source, never adopted as our own finding.

Corrections

Spotted an error or a stale value? Every register row links its source — if our row disagrees with the listing, the listing wins. Open an issue in the repo or note it in COORDINATION.md; corrections are logged with their source.
