Privacy

Version 1.0 · 20 November 2026·Applies to this entire site

The short version: this site sets no cookies, loads no third-party trackers, and sends nothing to ad networks. Measurement is first-party, minimal and under your control.

What is stored on your device

  • ks_session_v1 — a random session identifier (e.g. s-m1x2k8-ab73f2q9). Not derived from you; meaningless outside this browser.
  • ks_events_v1 — a ring buffer of up to 500 events you generated (page views, link clicks, searches), so the Measurement page can show them. Clear it any time with the button on that page.

What is never collected

  • Cookies of any kind — session or advertising.
  • Raw IP addresses, device fingerprints, or precise location.
  • Names, emails or payment data (this site has no accounts and no forms today).
  • Cross-site browsing history or data shared with third parties.

If an analytics endpoint is enabled

Today the endpoint is unset (FLG-006) — events stay in your browser. If a self-hosted endpoint is deployed later, it will receive: the event name, timestamp, path, truncated referrer, session id and the event detail shown on the Measurement page. The shipped endpoint code discards IP addresses beyond transient request handling, aggregates by day, and stores no raw identifiers. This page will be updated before any endpoint goes live.

Amazon's role

Product links go to Amazon. When you click a sponsored link, Amazon's own Privacy Notice governs what happens on their side — including their Associates tracking mechanisms. Amazon requires associates to disclose their use of cookies and third-party technologies; that requirement is why this page and the site's compliance notes exist.

Your choices

  • Block JavaScript → the site works; measurement simply doesn't run.
  • Clear site data → the local session id and event buffer are deleted.
  • Use an ad/tracker blocker → nothing here will break; we load zero third-party scripts.

Changes

Material changes (a new endpoint, a subscribe form, a new data field) get published here before they ship, and flagged in the flag list while pending review. Related decisions: SG-12 (endpoint hosting), SG-30 (reader capture).
