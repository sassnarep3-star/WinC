A working draft for owner review. It describes five Amazon listings and what could and could not be confirmed. It makes no performance, revenue, or commission claim.

What is confirmed

  • Marketplace. The destinations are amazon.com; the owner's location is the Netherlands (F-04).
  • Commission, competition, demand, trend, conversion. Not analysed (see the Analysis page).
