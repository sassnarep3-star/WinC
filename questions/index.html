Q-02 Are the five tokens truncated ASINs, mistyped ASINs, or an identifier from some other system?

Decision it unblocks
Whether we can auto-correct anything at all, and how the collector should validate incoming links.
What we have
A reproducible shape measurement: every token is 9 characters and mixed-case (B04G1MEwY, B02NbDxp3, B00hJ57DV, B0c8moW59, B0f3GKalj).
What we lack
An authoritative statement of the ASIN length rule that we were able to open (Seller Central help was not reachable here), and any evidence about how the tokens were captured.
How to find out
Two steps: (1) note where the links came from - a clipboard pass, a spreadsheet, or a link tool; a lost character across all five usually means a copy/paste or export artefact. Q-11 Where would event data be collected, on what legal basis, and for how long?

Decision it unblocks
Whether the click counter posts anywhere, what the privacy page must say, and whether an EU-focused site needs a consent step.
What we have
GitHub Pages is static - it cannot run a collector. Participation Requirements expect cookie/pixel disclosure (SRC-02 §3(e)); GitHub logs visitor IP addresses (SRC-09). Amazon's own text names the ePrivacy Directive and GDPR as applicable law (SRC-02 §3(a)), which matters because the operator is Netherlands-based.
What we lack
A host we control, a retention policy, a processing basis, and a decision on whether aggregate counting is worth the disclosure burden at all.
How to find out
Choose one: (a) keep counting local-only, (b) run the supplied collector on a VPS with 30-day retention, (c) adopt a self-hosted privacy analytics product. Then write the privacy page from the choice, not the other way round.
Who can answer
Site owner
Blocks
