Automation roadmap · gated, not autopilot

Stage A · compliant research intake

Collect only permitted source data.

Use authorized Amazon APIs only after account eligibility and current license terms are confirmed; do not scrape product pages. Store source URL, retrieval time, permitted fields and expiry/update rules with every fact. Identify topic opportunities from verified Search Console query reports and clearly label this as observed site demand, not general-market popularity.

Stage B · decision support

Rank evidence coverage before opportunity.

Have the system surface gaps, price-history availability, reader question fit and competitor/source evidence with confidence labels and citations. Do not calculate earnings potential until authorized reports support it; keep factors and thresholds reviewable rather than presenting a black-box score.

Stage C · human-reviewed content

Draft, cite, and stop at approval.

Generate outlines and comparison tables only from a versioned source register. Automatic checks can catch missing links, stale timestamps, disclosures and sitemap/feed omissions. A human must approve factual claims, product mapping, claims of testing and every purchase link.

Stage D · measure responsibly

Separate site behavior from merchant reports.

Self-host aggregate first-party events only after backend and privacy decisions. Import authorized Amazon order/click reports as a separate dated ledger; do not claim to observe customer-level conversions or predict revenue from sparse data.

Reader capture · proposal only

Not live: no email address, survey endpoint or reader database is configured. Decide whether to start with a low-data feedback prompt, optional newsletter opt-in, or neither; then define consent language, purpose, retention, access and deletion before building it. Avoid asking for email without a secure destination and reviewed privacy notice.
