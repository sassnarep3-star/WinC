Executive summary · 20 November 2026

A polished front door.
A careful gate behind it.

The repository started as a minimal README. It now has a publication-style affiliate research site with a buyer-first homepage, transparent evidence pages, and an explicit hold on unverified product links.

What was built

Publication architecture

Responsive brand and landing page, question-led field notes, product shortlist, editorial standards, suggestion board, open questions, measurement ledger, disclosure and flagged review pages. Shared masthead disclosure and footer navigation make the research surfaces reachable.

What was verified

Policy references, not product claims

Official Amazon Associates Agreement and policies, Amazon Associates link/ASIN help, and FTC endorsement guidance are linked. Their requirements are translated into review gates; they do not validate any submitted product or account.

What remains unknown

Product and account evidence

Five `link.amazon` references were provided and preserved. Their exact destination and compliance remain unresolved; they are not called invalid. Product titles, intended ASIN mappings, prices, reviews, features, commissions, tracking reports and enrollment remain unverified.

What is deliberately absent

No fabricated data or fake automation

No purchase buttons, product rankings, price claims, earnings forecast, conversion score, click counter, conversion tracker, analytics endpoint or API integration is claimed live. GitHub Pages is static and can't host a server endpoint.

Recommended decision path

  1. Verify one product reference at a time in the enrolled Amazon Associates workflow; record intended ASIN/listing/date/source separately from observed redirect target.
  2. Confirm tracking ID association and check the authorized tracking report for the single test link.
  3. Confirm actual product category and reader audience before turning draft air-fryer ideas into product-specific articles.
  4. Publish one sourced guide with human sign-off, disclosure, canonical URL, sitemap entry and RSS item.
  5. Choose and privacy-review a backend before enabling event collection; then assess Search Console and first-party report data.

Key official sources for review

  • Amazon Associates Operating Agreement
  • Amazon Associates Program Policies
  • Amazon Associates product link / ASIN help
  • FTC Endorsement Guides Q&A
