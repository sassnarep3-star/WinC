Publishing cadence proposal

Low-risk starting cadence: publish one fully sourced guide every two weeks for six weeks, then review Search Console queries and reader feedback before setting a long-term pace. This is an operational proposal, not a forecast or traffic claim.
  1. Confirm product category and intended listing evidence first.
  2. Research one clearly phrased buyer question using official product documentation and independent primary sources where relevant.
  3. Have an editor verify every factual sentence, citation, disclosure placement, and link destination.
  4. Add the final canonical URL to the sitemap and feed only on publication; record the date and evidence.
  5. Review real Search Console queries after access and enough impressions exist. Do not infer intent from no-data periods.
