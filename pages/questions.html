A decision is only as good as its evidence

What do we know—and what don't we?

Below is the minimum discovery checklist before decisions about products, claims, publishing and measurement can be considered reasonable.

Evidence boundary: verified project inputs are the selected tracking ID and five submitted URL strings. Their enrollment status, destinations, products and category were not independently established.
Identity & catalog

What are the five products?

Known: five references were submitted. Unknown: brand, model, category, intended ASIN, canonical listing. Discover: resolve and inspect each listing in the enrolled Associates workflow; save a dated confirmation and official page URL.

Links & tracking

Are these the intended Special Links?

Known: selected tracking ID supplied as shopnow047c-20. Unknown: tag association, redirect target and reporting result. Discover: generate/test one link per product and check the authorized Associates report.

Audience & intent

Who is the reader, and what choice are they making?

Known: buyer-question style is requested. Unknown: country/storefront, audience needs, budget range, cooking habits and accessibility considerations. Discover: interview a small sample of readers and validate query evidence after Search Console is live.

Product claims

Which features, prices and review facts can be stated?

Known: no product facts were confirmed. Unknown: dimensions, capacity, functions, current price, rating, review count and commission. Discover: use current official listing/API data under applicable policy; timestamp price data if permitted. Leave unsupported fields blank.

Research & testing

Is this a researched comparison or a first-person test?

Known: no hands-on evidence was provided. Unknown: access to units, test protocol and repeatability. Discover: decide whether to obtain products; document method, date, conditions and limitations. Otherwise publish an evidence-led comparison without implying testing.

Economics

What outcomes have actually been reported?

Known: none in the repository. Unknown: eligible sessions, outbound clicks, ordered items, approved earnings and commissions. Discover: use authorized first-party reports with date ranges and provenance. Do not forecast revenue or calculate a conversion score from empty data.

Measurement & privacy

What may be collected, and where will it live?

Known: current site is static GitHub Pages. Unknown: chosen endpoint, retention, legal basis, consent requirements, regional scope and access controls. Discover: choose a host and have privacy/compliance reviewed before collection.

Operations

Who owns updates and sign-off?

Known: a publishing workflow is requested. Unknown: accountable editor, review cadence, account status, budget and rollback owner. Discover: name a role, approval checklist, source archive and correction process before automating publication.

Reader capture

Should the publication collect emails or feedback?

Known: no reader capture mechanism is configured. Unknown: desired reader benefit, destination, consent wording, retention, deletion, privacy notice and owner. Discover: choose no capture, anonymous feedback or an opt-in newsletter only after the data purpose and compliant handling path are agreed.

Minimum go/no-go sequence

  1. Confirm storefront, enrollment and each intended product listing.
  2. Verify a single tagged link destination and first-party tracking report.
  3. Approve claims and disclosures against current official Amazon and FTC guidance.
  4. Choose the content scope and test one human-reviewed guide.
  5. Only then decide whether to scale automation and add analytics infrastructure.
