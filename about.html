About ShopNow Kitchen: a publication, not a dashboard

We compare countertop appliances on checkable specs and human buyer questions. Here is who writes, how we check, and what “published” means here.

By the ShopNow Kitchen Desk · Updated 2026-11-20

Authorship

Every page is bylined the ShopNow Kitchen Desk (editorial team). We do not invent personal credentials, test kitchens, or hands-on reviews. If we ever test a product, the page will say what was tested, when, and what was observed — with photos. Until then, we compare manufacturer/Amazon-listed specs and say so.

Methodology

  1. Start from the submitted link; record intent and observation in separate fields (never merged).
  2. Confirm specs on Amazon’s own listing pages; link every table row to its source.
  3. Leave unverified cells blank and flag them (see Flagged for Review).
  4. Run the compliance gate (scripts/check_site.py) before publishing.
  5. Measure with the five defined events; fill the ledger only from first-party reports.

Brand, seasons, and clusters

  • Brand: ShopNow Kitchen — warm paper, ink text, terracotta + green. No Amazon logos or product images.
  • Season: “Countertop Cooking” (everyday meals). Seasonal angles (grilling, holiday baking) appear as guides, not banners.
  • Clusters: Air Fryers (3 products, 3 guides) and Meat Thermometers (2 products, 1 guide). Every guide links to its category.

Publishing cadence

New guides are added to the existing sitemap and RSS feed in the same build. The next topic is chosen from measured Search Console queries — not instinct — once Search Console data exists (see Measurement).
