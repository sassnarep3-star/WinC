Questions readers ask about this one

How many probes come with it?

The listing names 3 ultra-thin probes and a hub with 5 sensors, reporting over WiFi, with probes stated heat safe to 1000F. It also lists BBQ, grill, oven, smoker and air fryer among its uses.

Does it need an app?

The headline feature on the listing is WiFi reporting, so the reading arrives on a phone - which means the app, the hub and the router are part of the product. If you would rather not install an app, the instant-read in this shop is the other shape.

What does 'unlimited range' mean?

It is a marketing phrase on the listing. What it means is that the hub reports over your network rather than over a short radio link; how far the hub can sit from the cooker is a question about your router and your house, not a published number.
