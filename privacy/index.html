Privacy

Nothing is sent off your device by us

This is a static site. There is no analytics tag, no advertising pixel, no social widget and no third-party script: the two JavaScript files are ours, are same-origin, and load nothing else. Our own click counter keeps its totals in your browser's local storage and posts them nowhere — the collector ships switched off.

If you turn the collector on

The plan, in SG-06, is an aggregate counter on infrastructure the operator runs, with a short retention window, no IP capture, and no per-visitor identifier. Two of those are not merely taste: the Associates terms bar associating a sub-tag or programme reporting with a specific end user, and they bar generating clicks or sessions artificially — so the collector is written to count events, never to invent them, and to refuse a visitor key outright.

Readers in the EU: this site has no consent banner because it sets no cross-site trackers; the disclosure here is the notice. That position depends on the collector staying off, and it is an open question (Q-11) for the operator, not a legal conclusion.
