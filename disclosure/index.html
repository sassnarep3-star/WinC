Who pays for what, here

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. That sentence is the wording the Associates Program Operating Agreement requires, and we reproduce it without changing it. It appears at the top of every page, again in the footer of every page, and again at the end of every guide.

WhatWhere it appearsWhy
The required sentenceStrip under the masthead on every page; repeated in the footer; repeated on each guideThe agreement requires it to be clearly and prominently stated on the site, and a reader should not have to scroll to find out who may be paid
A link-level labelImmediately beside each store link, reading “${esc(CFG.monetization.link_level_label)}”Amazon's own help separates the site-wide sentence from the label that sits next to a link
<code>rel="sponsored"</code>Every store link's markupGoogle documents <code>sponsored</code> as the value for paid placements; it costs us no traffic, because attribution is carried by the tag parameter, not by a search engine
<code>tag=${esc(CFG.monetization.tracking_id)}</code>Every store link's URLThe agreement permits only the tagged link formats Amazon provides, and an untagged link does not accrue

There are currently no store links to any product on this site, because no product is confirmed. The rules above are implemented in the generator and asserted by the gate so that the first real link is compliant on the day it appears.

What we will not do to earn a commission

  • No prices, availability, star ratings or review text — only an Amazon-served link or the approved API may carry those, and we have neither.
  • No price alerts, countdowns, “#1 best” badges, or invented test results.
  • No redirect through an intermediate page: a link that sends a reader on without a click of their own disqualifies the purchase and hides the destination.
  • No paid search on Amazon's own trademarks, and no Special Links inside an RSS item body or in unsolicited email.
  • No reward, perk or donation incentive for clicking.
