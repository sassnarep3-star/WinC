Who made this

Byline: Crisp Report desk. Drafted with machine assistance inside a repository session; a named human must review and sign off before this line changes to 'reviewed by'. Until then the site says who made it and how, which is the answer to Google's 'Who / How / Why' questions (SRC-08).

There is no named human author yet, and we will not invent one. A fake reviewer would be a false identity in the masthead and a misleading endorsement in the copy at the same time, so every page here stops at method — what to check, which field to read, which claim is unverifiable — rather than asserting a personal test nobody performed. When the operator supplies a name, authorship.person_byline and review_required in site.config.json change, and each guide gains a reviewed by line.

How we work

  1. A claim enters a data file, not a page, with a source id attached.
  2. The generator writes the HTML; nobody hand-edits a published number.
  3. The gate refuses the build if a cell lacks a source, a disclosure is missing, a store link is untagged or unlabelled, or a price or rating pattern appears anywhere.
  4. Anything that cannot be confirmed stays visibly blank with a reason, and gets a row on the flagged page.
  5. Product rows carry a re-check date; older than thirty days fails the build until someone re-reads or removes them.

Corrections

Log: data/corrections.json — schema published, no entries yet. A correction gets a row within one working day of confirmation, on the page it belongs to, with what was wrong and what is true.

Contact

The route that exists today is the repository: github.com/sassnarep3-star/WinC — open an issue for a correction, an accepted decision from the ideas board, or the answers to the blocking questions. We deliberately do not print an email address we have not been given.
